# Message register

Use this register for text sent to someone for a decision or a reply: email,
Slack, WhatsApp, issues, review comments and short public messages. The reader is
the recipient, and the text serves their next step.

## Outgoing messages

Make the ask, finding or decision easy to find near the start. Open naturally for
the recipient and channel: a greeting such as "Hi Alex" is fine, followed by the
point. Keep greetings, thanks and closings when they suit the relationship. Remove
stock filler that adds neither information nor appropriate tone.

Write directly to a peer. Use real component names, concrete examples and measured
numbers where available. Keep necessary context, but cut flattery and invented
enthusiasm. Do not imitate the author's clipped shorthand from an assistant
conversation.

Match the language, channel and recipient. Work messages are composed and
unembellished. Personal or committee messages can be warmer and informal, with a
sparse emoji when natural for the recipient. Personality is welcome; forced
bluntness is not. State real uncertainty plainly rather than hiding it behind
"perhaps" or "it seems".
